V-JEPA vs VideoMAE on UCF101

Protocol

  • Official UCF101 splits 1, 2, and 3; seed 42.
  • Five classes; 60 train and 20 test videos per class and split.
  • Identical 16-frame, 224×224 clips and frozen mean-pooled backbones.
  • Same StandardScaler + LogisticRegression; MPS timings after warm-up.

Key findings

  • Accuracy: V-JEPA 0.990 ± 0.010; VideoMAE 0.850 ± 0.044.
  • V-JEPA gains 14.0 percentage points in accuracy and 14.1 points in macro-F1.
  • VideoMAE is 3.07× faster (0.484 versus 1.486 s/video).
  • VideoMAE's hardest class is WalkingWithDog (F1 0.706 ± 0.078).

Scope: these conclusions apply to this balanced five-class frozen-feature protocol, not full 101-class fine-tuning.
